Characters

Players choose unique heroes, each with their own abilities, strengths, and roles. Some heroes specialize in dealing damage, others in protecting teammates, and some focus on controlling the battlefield. Picking the right combination of heroes and coordinating their abilities is key to outplaying the enemy team and achieving objectives.
