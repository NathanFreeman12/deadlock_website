Deadlock

What is it?

Deadlock is a multiplayer team-based game by Valve that combines shooter gameplay with strategic MOBA elements. Players choose unique heroes and work together to control the map, fight opponents, and complete objectives. The goal is to outplay the opposing team through teamwork and strategy to win the match.

Laning

There are three lanes:

  1. Greenwich (Green)
  2. Broadway (Blue)
  3. York (Yellow)

At the start of the match, six players are divided into three lanes in teams of two. Each duo focuses on their assigned lane, where they eliminate enemy bots to earn money and gain experience. Players also compete against opposing heroes, protect their lane structures, and push toward the enemy side. The goal of the laning phase is to build resources, apply pressure, and set up advantages for the mid game through smart positioning and teamwork

Objectives

Objectives are key structures and points on the map that teams fight to control. Capturing or destroying them gives advantages like map control, resources, and paths to push deeper into enemy territory. Securing objectives is often more important than kills, as they help your team work toward winning the match.

There are multiple objectives such as:

    Patron: The final boss located in the center of the enemy base.
    Guardians: The initial defensive structures in each lane.
    Walkers: The second set of defensive structures in each lane.
    Defenders: Smaller, automated defensive units.
    Troopers: The basic lane minions.

Other objectives that give you a big advantage

    Mid Boss: A powerful neutral boss in the center of the map that drops buffs.
    Urn: A special objective that can be carried to a drop-off point for team-wide souls.
